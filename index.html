About Us

About IZON AI

IZON AI is a software and AI solutions company based in Valsad, Gujarat, India. We design and build AI agents, voice AI, automation systems and custom software for businesses that want to run leaner and respond faster.

Our work spans AI agents and chatbots, voice AI and calling automation, business process automation, custom machine learning, and full software development — including management platforms for gyms, clinics, schools and retail businesses.

We focus on practical, working systems: technology that integrates into how a business already operates, rather than generic tools that need to be worked around.
